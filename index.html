Working at the intersection of topology, geometry, and quantum matter. My research focuses on unconventional superconductivity, proximitized pairing in heterostructures, quantum magnetism, and engineered quantum materials, combining analytical and numerical methods toward experimentally relevant predictions.

Research Areas

What I work on

01 ⬡

Non-trivial topology and quantum geometry in materials

I work to find materials with interesting non-trivial band structures that can host exotic states in superconductors and quantum materials.

02 ◉

Unconventional superconductivity and hybrid heterostructures

Majorana zero modes, topology with magnetism and spin-orbit coupling, and phase transitions in low-dimensional systems.

03 ⇅

Open quantum systems

Studying non-trivial bath coupling and decoherence in quantum systems, especially in condensed matter settings.

04 ∿

Computational and data-driven physics

Exact diagonalization, Monte Carlo methods, and machine learning for phase identification and experimental analysis.
