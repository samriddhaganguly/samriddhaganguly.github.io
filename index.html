Research areas

What I work on

01

Topology & quantum geometry

Investigating nontrivial band structures, topological invariants, and geometric response in condensed matter systems.

02

Unconventional superconductivity

Studying proximitized pairing, emergent edge states, and superconducting heterostructures in topological settings.

03

Quantum magnetism

Exploring chiral magnons, layered antiferromagnets, magnetic phase transitions, and nontrivial excitations.

04

Open quantum systems

Understanding decoherence, dissipation, and driven dynamics in systems coupled to environments and baths.

Research & Publications

01

Topological quantum matter

I study nontrivial band structures, topology, and quantum geometry in condensed matter systems, with emphasis on realistic low-dimensional materials and excitations.

02

Unconventional superconductivity

Research focuses on proximitized pairing, edge states, and magnetically coupled systems relevant to topological and engineered superconductors.

03

Quantum magnetism

Exploration of chiral magnons, antiferromagnetic phases, and symmetry-driven magnetic excitations in low-dimensional lattices.

04

Open quantum systems

Current work also covers dissipation, decoherence, and nonequilibrium effects in systems coupled to baths or fluctuating environments.
