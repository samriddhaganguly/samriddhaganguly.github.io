Research & Publications

Exploring topological phases, unconventional superconductivity, and quantum magnetism — and the peer-reviewed work that has come out of it.

Current direction

The present line

I am working on superconducting–ferromagnetic heterostructures, with a focus on naturally occurring systems and interfacial pairing, while also learning about K-theoretic classification of topological phases and symmetry-based descriptions in quantum matter.

Areas of focus

Research topics

01

Topological quantum matter & magnetism

I study topological phases in both electronic and magnetic systems, including Chern insulators, symmetry-protected phases, and chiral magnonic modes.

  • Chern insulators
  • Topological magnons
  • Honeycomb antiferromagnets
  • Thermal Hall effect
02

Topological superconductivity

I am fascinated by unconventional superconducting phases where topology protects gapless boundary modes and Majorana-like edge states.

  • Majorana zero modes
  • p-wave superconductivity
  • Spin-orbit coupling
  • Phase transitions
03

Hybrid heterostructures

Proximity effects at interfaces between superconductors and quantum materials are a rich playground for emergent physics.

  • Andreev reflection
  • Van der Waals systems
  • Altermagnets
  • Flat-band physics
04

Computational & data-driven physics

I use exact diagonalization, MPS/DMRG, Monte Carlo, and machine-learning-inspired analysis to classify phases and parse experimental data.

  • ED and MCMC
  • Phase classification
  • Deep learning
  • Numerical modeling
Methods

Methods & tools

Topological methods

Berry phase, Chern numbers, bulk-boundary correspondence, and symmetry-protected topological invariants.

Many-body techniques

Second quantization, Bogoliubov formalism, path integrals, diagrams, and mean-field approximations.

Open quantum systems

Density matrices, Lindblad dynamics, entropy, and measurement theory in non-equilibrium settings.

Computational tools

Exact diagonalization, DMRG/MPS, Monte Carlo, Python, NumPy, SciPy, and QuTiP.
